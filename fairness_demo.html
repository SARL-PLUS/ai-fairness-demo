🐱 AI Fairness Demo: Cat vs. Dog Adoption 🐶

Can an AI be fair to both groups? Adjust the thresholds and find out.

Scenario: A pet shelter uses an AI to predict which animals will be adopted within 30 days. Animals predicted "likely adopted" get premium website placement (more visibility = more chances).

The catch: Dogs have a 🐶 70% adoption rate, but cats have a 🐱 40% adoption rate. The AI assigns each animal a "adoption score" from 0–100. You set the threshold — animals above it get premium placement.

Your challenge: Can you set thresholds so that the AI is fair to both cats and dogs? Try to make all three fairness criteria green. Spoiler: you can't.
